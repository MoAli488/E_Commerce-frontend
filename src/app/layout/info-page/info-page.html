Domestic orders typically arrive within 2–4 business days. You will receive real-time notifications in your account under "My Orders".

We gladly accept returns of unworn, unwashed items with tags attached within 30 days of receiving your package.

How do I cancel or modify an existing order?

You may cancel an order directly while it remains in "pending" status from your Account page. Once in "preparing" or "shipped", please reach out to customer support.
